# 微信视频号下载

Native Android app. Author: nihaomaaa (https://github.com/nihaomaaa). Preserve paste/share → resolve → download → MediaStore and three tabs. Apple app design, functional liquid-glass navigation and progressive edge blur. Keep both the website and direct Tencent Yuanbao resolvers selectable.
